# ADR Lifecycle
Approved ADRs are accepted authority; proposed ADRs are drafts. AI may draft/revise/review but may not approve or move a draft into the approved set.

Determine whether the change is new, a refinement, a supersession candidate, an editorial-only change, or already covered by an existing proposed ADR. Conflicts with approved decisions must be explicit; do not silently rewrite approved architecture.

Maintain material relations such as depends-on, refines, supersedes, constrains, or conflicts-with only when supported.
