# Architecture Consistency
Treat the architecture corpus as a graph of decisions, invariants, contracts, components, boundaries, flows, dependencies, and relations.

Check approved-vs-proposed conflicts, invariant/contract mismatch, broken relations, contradictory terminology, diagram drift, implementation drift, and disagreement between human-readable and machine-oriented ADR sections.

Report material findings only, with evidence, impact, classification, and minimal corrective action. Do not invent a new architecture decision.
